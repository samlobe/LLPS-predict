Training data notes (LLPS-predict)

This repository includes sequence resources used for LLPS model training and analysis:
- model_development/compile_IDR_drivers/IDRs.fasta
- model_development/compile_IDR_drivers/drivers.csv
- model_development/compile_IDR_drivers/members.csv

Training scripts consume embedding tables (for example driver/non-driver embeddings) and train logistic models.

If regenerating embeddings from FASTA:
1) Build FASTA matching your target sequence IDs.
2) Use ESM extraction from the ESM repository:
   https://github.com/facebookresearch/esm
   script: scripts/extract.py
3) Convert extracted embeddings into CSV tables expected by training scripts.
